## Version 3 — Long

Building an AI journaling app means getting the AI right. Not just "working" — right.

I rewrote Echo's entire personality system. Here's what changed:

- It references specific things you wrote, not vague summaries
- It can disagree with you (respectfully) when your logic doesn't hold up
- Weekly digests now pull from all your notes instead of cherry-picking one
- Responses stay short and direct — no more walls of text

The hardest part wasn't the code. It was figuring out the line between a useful companion and an annoying one. Echo shouldn't be a therapist, a coach, or a yes-machine. Just a friend who remembers what you said.

Still in beta on Android. More coming soon.
