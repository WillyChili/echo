## Post 3: Long

> Building an AI journaling app as a solo dev has been the most intense project I've worked on. Here's where Echo is at:
>
> - Write notes by voice or text, in English or Spanish
> - Echo reads everything you write and builds context over time
> - Ask it anything about your own notes. It answers from your words, not generic advice
> - Weekly digest with a personal reflection section that references real things you wrote
> - Upgraded the AI to catch patterns across entries and point out when your actions don't match your goals
>
> The hardest part was making the AI feel like a companion, not a chatbot. No motivational quotes. No "that's great!" after everything you say. Echo will respectfully disagree with you if your reasoning is weak.
>
> Privacy-first. No ads ever. Free tier with 10 chats/day and unlimited notes.
>
> It's live on the Play Store if you want to try it: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.willychili.echo
