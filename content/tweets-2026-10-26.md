## Version 3 — Long

Dev update on Echo, the AI journaling app I've been building solo.

Recent work:

Rewrote Echo's entire personality. The old version was too polite, too generic. The new one references specific things from your notes, disagrees with you when your logic doesn't hold up, and keeps responses to 2-4 sentences. No bullet lists. No "great question!" No motivational filler.

Rebuilt the weekly digest. It now has a "Reflexion" section that pulls concrete topics from your entries. Not "you seem to be going through a period of change" but "you mentioned three times this week you're postponing the dentist appointment." Took about 8 iterations to get the tone right.

Shipped voice input that runs your transcript through Claude to add punctuation without changing a single word. You speak freely, it cleans up the formatting. That's it.

Cleaned up the codebase. Removed dead code, orphaned translations, obsolete files. The kind of work nobody sees but makes everything faster to ship.

Landing page is live with Play Store download. One dev, no funding, building in public.

If you journal and want an AI that actually knows your context: Echo on Google Play.
