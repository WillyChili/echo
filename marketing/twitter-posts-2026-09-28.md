## Day 2 - Building in Public

### Short

Echo runs on Claude AI + Supabase + React/Capacitor. One-person team. Shipping weekly. The AI has three modes: warm, direct, curious. You pick how it talks to you.

### Medium

Building Echo as a solo dev has been a ride.

Stack: Claude AI (Anthropic) for the brain, Supabase for data + auth, React + Capacitor for the Android app, Railway for the backend.

Latest update: rewrote Echo's entire AI personality. Three tone modes you can choose from:
- Warm: patient, present, no drama
- Direct: get to the point, skip the feelings
- Curious: asks you the right questions

Also shipped weekly digests that actually reference what you wrote. Not generic "you had a productive week" filler.

### Long

Week in review for Echo development.

Spent most of this sprint rewriting how Echo thinks. The AI personality went through about 8 iterations. The problem: the weekly digest kept giving vague, generic reflections. "You seem focused on personal growth." No. Tell me I mentioned calling the accountant three times and never did it.

So I rebuilt the Reflexion section from scratch. Now it pulls specific topics, events, and tasks from your actual notes. If you wrote about a pasta recipe on Tuesday and a work meeting on Thursday, that's what shows up. Not abstract themes.

Also refined Echo's voice. It can now disagree with you. If your notes say one thing and you're telling Echo another, it'll call that out. Respectfully. That's the kind of AI companion I actually want.

Tech stack for the curious:
- Claude Haiku 4.5 (Anthropic) for all AI
- Supabase (Postgres + auth)
- React + Vite frontend
- Capacitor for native Android
- Railway for hosting
- Resend for email digests

All built by one person. Shipping every week.
