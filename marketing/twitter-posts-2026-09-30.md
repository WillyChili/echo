## Version 3 — Long

Been building Echo for months. Here's where it's at.

Echo is an AI journaling app. You write daily notes (text or voice), and Echo becomes a companion that actually knows your context.

What makes it different:
- It reads your notes and references specific things you wrote. Not summaries. The actual topics, tasks, events.
- Weekly digest that reviews your week with concrete takeaways.
- Voice input with AI-powered punctuation cleanup. Just talk.
- Bilingual (English + Spanish).
- Free tier: 10 chats/day, unlimited notes, weekly digest.

The hardest part was getting the AI to stop sounding like a therapist. I rewrote the personality three times until it stopped saying things like "I notice a pattern of growth in your journey." Now it just asks if you called the accountant.

Live on Android. Building in public.

play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.willychili.echo
