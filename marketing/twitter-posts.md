## Day 3 - The Product Vision Angle

### Short
Most note apps store what you write. Echo understands it. That one line took me months to figure out, and now it drives every decision I make on the product.

### Medium
The pitch for Echo is simple: think out loud, Echo remembers.

Write a note about your day. Next week, ask Echo what you were stressed about. It knows because it read your words, not because you tagged or organized anything.

No folders. No tags. No setup. Just write and go.

Voice input in English and Spanish. Weekly AI-generated digests. Free on Android.

### Long
Here's why I'm building Echo instead of using an existing app.

I tried Notion, Day One, even plain text files. They all have the same problem: they're storage, not understanding. You write something and it sits there until you scroll past it months later.

Echo takes a different approach. It reads everything you write, builds context over time, and lets you talk to it. Not in a weird sci-fi way. More like a friend who actually listened when you were talking.

"What did I say about that project last month?" — Echo pulls it up.
"Summarize my week." — Echo writes a digest from your actual notes.
"I'm stuck on a decision." — Echo reminds you what you said about it before.

Voice and text. English and Spanish. Customizable personality (Pro). Weekly digests by email (Pro).

Free tier is generous: unlimited notes, 10 chats/day, one weekly digest. Building for Android first.
