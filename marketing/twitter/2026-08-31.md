## Version 3 - Long

Small update on Echo, the AI journaling app I've been building:

The landing page now links directly to the Play Store. Sounds simple but it means the whole flow works end to end: you land on the site, you download, you start writing.

What Echo does: you write notes (voice or text), and an AI companion learns from them over time. It doesn't just store your words, it understands context. You can chat with it and it answers from your own notes.

Recent work:
- Rewrote the AI personality to feel more natural and direct
- Weekly digest now references actual content from your notes instead of generic summaries
- Voice input cleans up punctuation automatically
- Bilingual support (English/Spanish)

Still in beta, still free. Built with React, Capacitor, and Supabase.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.willychili.echo
