## Version 3: Long

I've been building Echo, a personal AI journaling app for Android, and this was one of the biggest update cycles so far.

What changed:

1. Completely rewrote Echo's AI identity. It now references specific things you wrote (tasks, events, ideas), not vague interpretations. It can disagree with you. It follows up on past entries naturally. Three personality modes: Warm, Direct, and Curious.

2. Rebuilt the weekly digest from scratch. Added a "Reflexion" section that covers your whole week, pulls concrete topics from your notes, and offers one practical suggestion. No abstract themes. No assumptions about what you're feeling.

3. Upgraded the voice input flow. New mic button with visual feedback (mint idle state, spinner while processing). Speech transcripts get cleaned up by AI before saving so your notes read clean even when you ramble.

4. Small UX wins: settings save silently in the background, chat jumps to the latest message instantly, notes sort correctly by date.

Echo is free with 10 chats/day and unlimited notes. Building this solo with Claude AI, Supabase, and React + Capacitor.
