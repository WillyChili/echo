## Version 3 — Long

Most AI apps forget you the moment you close the tab.

I'm building Echo, a journaling app where you write daily notes and talk to an AI companion that actually knows your context. It reads what you wrote last Tuesday and brings it up when it's relevant. It notices when what you say now doesn't match what you wrote before. It sends you a weekly digest that reflects on your real week, not a template.

Recent work: rewrote the AI identity to be more direct and less generic, added voice input with bilingual speech-to-text (EN/ES), and built a weekly "Reflexion" section that reads like a message from a friend who was paying attention.

Still early. Still building. Android only for now.
