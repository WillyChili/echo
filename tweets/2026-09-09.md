## Version 3 — Long

Building in public update:

Echo is an AI journaling app I've been working on. You write notes (voice or text, in English or Spanish), and Echo, the AI companion, reads everything and builds context over time.

What makes it different from other AI chat apps:

- It doesn't start from zero every conversation. Echo carries your full journal as context.
- It references specific things you wrote. "You mentioned wanting to call the accountant last Tuesday. Did that happen?"
- It can disagree with you. If your notes say one thing and you're telling it another, it'll point that out calmly.
- No bullet lists, no motivational quotes, no "that's a great question!" It talks like a person.

We also built a weekly digest feature. Every week Echo sends you a structured reflection based on your notes. Not a summary, more like a friend saying "hey, you talked a lot about X this week, and here's what I noticed."

The landing page is live, the app is on the Play Store, and I'm still iterating on the AI personality every week.

If you journal or want to start, give it a try. It's free.
