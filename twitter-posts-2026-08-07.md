## Version 3 — Long

I've been building Echo — an AI journaling app for Android — and it just went live on the Play Store.

The idea is simple: you write notes about your day, your ideas, whatever is on your mind. Echo reads them, builds context over time, and becomes a companion that can actually talk back about your life using your own words.

What I shipped recently:
- Upgraded the AI model and rewrote Echo's entire personality. It now synthesizes across notes instead of just quoting them back at you. It catches patterns, progressions, even contradictions.
- Rebuilt the weekly digest from scratch. Multiple iterations to get the tone right — no motivational fluff, just concrete observations about what you actually wrote that week.
- Improved voice input: better mic UX, automatic punctuation cleanup, and a language toggle so you can switch between English and Spanish mid-note.
- Cleaned up the whole codebase. Removed dead features, unused files, and streamlined the settings page.

It's a solo project, built with React + Capacitor + Claude on the backend. Free tier gives you 10 chats/day and unlimited notes. Pro is $5/mo for unlimited everything.

Still in beta, still iterating. If you try it, I genuinely want to hear what you think. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.willychili.echo
