### Version 3 — Long

Been heads-down rebuilding how Echo thinks and talks.

Echo is a journaling app where you write daily notes and chat with an AI that actually knows your context. The problem with v1: the AI sounded like every other chatbot. "That's so great you're thinking about that!" No. Nobody wants that.

So I rewrote the entire identity system. Echo now references specific things you wrote: the project you mentioned, the conversation you had, the decision you're stuck on. It can disagree with you. It stays direct when you need advice and warm when you're reflecting.

The weekly digest got the same treatment. Instead of vague "you seem to be on a journey of growth" nonsense, it now pulls actual topics from your notes and gives you a concrete reflection. One section for key themes, one for a short personal take.
