## Version 3: Long

I've been working on Echo for a while now. It's an AI journaling app for Android where you write daily notes and talk to an AI that remembers what you've written.

Some things I focused on recently:

- Rewrote Echo's entire personality. It references specific things from your notes, not vague interpretations. If you said you needed to call your accountant last week, it asks if you did. No life coach talk.
- Built a weekly digest system that summarizes your key topics and gives you a short personal reflection. Went through about 10 iterations to get the tone right. Direct, respectful, no assumptions about what you're feeling.
- Voice input that cleans up your transcript with AI but never changes your words. Just adds punctuation.
- Cleaned up the whole first-time experience with loading skeletons and smooth transitions.
- Landing page is live, Play Store CTAs are active.

Free tier gives you 10 chats a day and unlimited notes. Pro is $5/mo for unlimited chats and a customizable companion.

Building in public. Solo dev, built with React, Capacitor, Claude Haiku, and Supabase.
