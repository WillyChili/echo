## Post 3: Long

Six months of building Echo in public, here's where it stands.

Echo is an AI journaling app. You write notes every day, and the AI companion reads all of them. When you chat, it knows what you wrote about last Tuesday. When it sends you a weekly digest, it talks about your actual week — the project you mentioned, the trip you planned, the recipe you tried.

Recent changes that took the most iteration:
- Rewrote the digest system ~10 times until it stopped sounding like a life coach and started sounding like a friend who pays attention
- Rebuilt Echo's core identity and response style — clear, direct, sometimes disagrees with you
- Added voice input with speech cleanup (Claude Haiku adds punctuation to your transcript without changing your words)
- Shipped the landing page with a dither shader background, bento feature grid, and Play Store CTAs

Solo dev, full stack — React + Capacitor for Android, Node/Express backend, Supabase for data, Claude Haiku for the AI. Using Claude Code for most of the development too.

Still early, still iterating. If you journal and want an AI that actually reads what you write, Echo is free on the Play Store.
