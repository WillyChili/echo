## Version 3: Long (thread-style, single post)

Most journal apps are just storage. You write, forget, repeat.

I wanted something different, so I built Echo.

You write a note (voice or text, EN/ES). Echo reads everything, learns your context, and actually talks back. Ask it about last week's ideas, what you said about that project, or what recipe you saved.

It also sends you a weekly digest with your key topics and a personal reflection.

Right now: free on Android, 10 chats/day, unlimited notes.

Been building this solo for months. Would love feedback.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.willychili.echo
