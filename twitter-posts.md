## Post 3 — Long

Building in public update on Echo.

Echo is a journaling app with an AI companion. You write notes by voice or text, and Echo reads everything, builds context over time, and lets you ask questions about your own journal. "What did I write about last week?" "What was that recipe?" It answers from your words, not the internet.

What shipped recently:
- Rewrote Echo's AI personality from scratch. Less generic chatbot, more real companion. It disagrees with you when your reasoning is off. It references specific things you wrote, not vague summaries.
- Weekly digest with a Reflexion section. Echo reviews your week and writes a short, honest take based on what you actually shared. No assumptions, no motivational quotes.
- Voice input cleanup. Speak naturally, Echo adds punctuation without changing your words.
- Landing page is live with a custom dither shader background.

It's free with 10 chats/day, unlimited notes, and a weekly digest. Pro unlocks unlimited chats and custom personality.

If you journal or want to start, give it a try: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.willychili.echo
