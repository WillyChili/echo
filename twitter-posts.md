## Day 2: Voice Input & Bilingual Speech

**Theme:** Echo now cleans up your voice notes with AI and supports seamless EN/ES switching, because thoughts don't wait for you to type.

### Short
> Added AI-powered voice cleanup to Echo. Speak naturally, get clean text. No more editing punctuation out of messy transcripts. Works in English and Spanish.

### Medium
> New in Echo: speak your thoughts, get clean notes.

> Voice-to-text always felt half-broken. You'd ramble into your phone and get a wall of text with no periods, no commas, nothing.

> Now Claude Haiku cleans up your transcript the moment you stop talking. Adds punctuation without changing a single word. Your voice, properly formatted.

> Also added an EN/ES language toggle right inside the note. Switch mid-thought if you need to.

> Building @JustEchoApp for people who think faster than they type.

### Long
> I've been building Echo, an AI journaling app, and voice input always bothered me.

> Speech-to-text on phones is decent at getting words right, but the output is a mess. No punctuation, no structure. You end up spending more time editing than you saved by not typing.

> So I built a cleanup layer. When you stop recording, Claude Haiku runs over the transcript and adds punctuation. Periods, commas, question marks. It never changes, removes, or adds words. Just formatting.

> The mic button now gives you visual feedback too. Mint color when idle, a spinner while the AI cleans your text, and a subtle press animation. Small details that make it feel right.

> Oh, and you can switch between English and Spanish with a pill toggle right inside the text area. No settings menu, no page reload. Just tap and talk.

> Capture fast. Echo handles the rest.
